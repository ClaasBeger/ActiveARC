Two measurement traps worth recording. A task's rate decides how many draws are needed to see it at all: at 0.03%% a 300-draw sample misses it nine times in ten, which is why the open count read 5, then 12, then 21 as sampling deepened. And the vendored ARC-AGI-2 verifiers import numpy, which fails inside a process forked from one that already loaded it — the loader swallows that and reports the task as having no verifier at all, so these runs spawn their workers instead.
